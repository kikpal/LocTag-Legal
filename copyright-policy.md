# LocTag Copyright and Takedown Policy

LocTag respects copyright and expects users to upload only material they own
or are authorized to use. Copyright complaints may be sent to
loctag.support@gmail.com and should include the information required by 17
U.S.C. 512(c)(3).

LocTag may remove or restrict identified material, notify the affected user,
accept a legally sufficient counter-notification, and terminate repeat
infringers when appropriate. A counter-notification may be forwarded to the
complainant as permitted by law.

Before public launch, the operator must register a designated DMCA agent with
the U.S. Copyright Office and replace this paragraph with the agent's complete
public contact information.
