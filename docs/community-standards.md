# LocTag Community Standards

Effective: September 21, 2026
Version: 2026-09-21

LocTag connects user-created LocTags and Guided Journey stops to real places.
That makes location safety, consent, and lawful access mandatory.

Contact: loctag.support@gmail.com

## Location safety

Do not publish a private home without authorization. Do not place LocTags at
schools, childcare locations, shelters, hospitals, treatment facilities,
military or critical infrastructure, restricted facilities, hazardous areas,
or places that require trespassing. Do not expose a person's sensitive or
real-time whereabouts.

## Prohibited content and behavior

LocTag prohibits stalking, harassment, threats, hate or abuse, sexual
exploitation, child sexual abuse or exploitation, trafficking, non-consensual
intimate content, doxxing, impersonation, scams, spam, malware, instructions
for serious wrongdoing, unlawful sales, and copyright or trademark
infringement.

## Reports and blocks

Users can report a LocTag and block a user in the app. Reporting immediately
hides the reported LocTag from the reporter and sends protected listing
evidence to the moderation queue. Blocking operates in both directions and
hides access and notifications between the accounts. A user may choose a
reversible block that preserves eligible relationships for restoration after
unblocking, or remove the blocked account from connections, groups, LocTags,
and Journeys where that option is offered. Reports are not public and do not
automatically delete content. The blocked account is not told who blocked it.

## Enforcement and appeals

LocTag may dismiss a report, hide a LocTag, temporarily suspend an account, or
permanently disable access. Moderation-hidden content stays inaccessible while
limited evidence is retained. One appeal is available for each eligible
moderation action, and an authorized moderator may uphold or overturn it.

## Urgent and illegal material

LocTag is not an emergency service. Contact local emergency services when
someone faces immediate danger. Suspected child sexual exploitation may be
reported to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children's
CyberTipline at https://report.cybertip.org/. Do not download, forward, or
redistribute suspected illegal imagery.

## Copyright

Copyright complaints should identify the protected work, the LocTag material,
the complainant's contact information, the required good-faith statements,
and a signature. Send complaints to loctag.support@gmail.com. LocTag intends
to register and publish a designated DMCA agent before public UGC launch.
