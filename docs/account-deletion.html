ACCOUNT AND ASSOCIATED DATA DELETION

Delete your LocTag account

You can request deletion inside the LocTag app or by email if you no longer have access to the app. Both pathways cover your LocTag account and its associated ordinary product data.

Request by email View in-app steps
30-day recovery period

A deletion request disables the account for ordinary use. Permanent deletion processing begins after the recovery deadline unless you cancel the request in the app before that deadline.

OPTION 1

Delete in the LocTag app

  1. Open LocTag and sign in to the account you want to delete.
  2. From Home, select Safety & Privacy.
  3. Select Delete My Account.
  4. Type DELETE MY ACCOUNT exactly as shown.
  5. Confirm that you understand the recovery deadline.
  6. Select Disable & Schedule Deletion, then confirm the request.

OPTION 2

Request deletion without the app

Send the request from the email address associated with your LocTag account. Include your LocTag username if you know it.

Email LocTag Support

Or email loctag.support@gmail.com with the subject LocTag account deletion request.

Protect your account.

Never send your password, verification code, password-reset link, access token, or payment information. LocTag may request limited information to verify that you control the account.

WHAT TO EXPECT

Deletion process

1

Request received

LocTag verifies the request when needed and schedules the account for deletion.

2

Account disabled

Ordinary account access, LocTags, and sharing access become unavailable during the 30-day recovery period.

3

Permanent processing

If the request is not cancelled before the displayed deadline, permanent deletion begins and cannot be undone.

ASSOCIATED DATA

Data deleted after the recovery period

Permanent processing removes the authentication account and ordinary product data associated with it, including applicable records in these categories:

  • Account profile, authentication identity, and account settings
  • LocTags, protected content, photos, drafts, and private Memories
  • Connections, groups, sharing relationships, and ordinary history
  • Guided Journeys, stops, participation, progress, and unlock records
  • No LocTag Zone requests, appeals, and private evidence associated with the account
  • Branded Trail pilot applications, campaigns, and account-associated aggregate records
  • Push tokens, notification preferences, and ordinary delivery records
  • Stored media and ordinary product activity owned by the account

LIMITED RETENTION

Data that may be retained

LocTag may retain limited private deletion-audit, safety, moderation, fraud-prevention, security, or legally required records only as long as reasonably necessary for those purposes. These records are not available for ordinary product use and cannot restore permanently deleted LocTags, content, or media.
