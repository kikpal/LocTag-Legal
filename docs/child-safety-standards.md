# LocTag Child Safety Standards

LocTag's initial United States release is for adults age 18 and older. LocTag
prohibits child sexual abuse and exploitation, grooming, sextortion,
trafficking, sexualization of minors, and child sexual abuse material.

Users can report content and block users in the app. LocTag reviews reports,
removes prohibited material, restricts responsible accounts, preserves
limited evidence, and reports apparent child sexual exploitation to the
appropriate authorities when required.

Child-safety point of contact: loctag.support@gmail.com

Suspected child sexual exploitation can be reported to the NCMEC CyberTipline:
https://report.cybertip.org/
