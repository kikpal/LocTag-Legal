# Delete Your LocTag Account

Last updated: September 1, 2026

This page explains how a LocTag user can request deletion of a LocTag account
and its associated data. LocTag is a United States location-based service for
adults age 18 and older.

## Delete your account in the LocTag app

1. Open LocTag and sign in to the account you want to delete.
2. From **Home**, select **Safety & Privacy**.
3. Select **Delete My Account**.
4. Type **DELETE MY ACCOUNT** exactly as shown.
5. Confirm that you understand the recovery deadline.
6. Select **Disable & Schedule Deletion**, then confirm the request.

The account is disabled immediately. You will be signed out, and your LocTags
and sharing access will become unavailable.

## Request deletion without access to the app

If you cannot access LocTag, send a deletion request from the email address
associated with your account:

[Email LocTag Support to request account deletion](mailto:loctag.support@gmail.com?subject=LocTag%20account%20deletion%20request)

In the message, include:

- The words **LocTag account deletion request**.
- The email address associated with the LocTag account.
- Your LocTag username, if known.

For security, do not send your password, password-reset link, verification
code, access token, or any payment information. LocTag may ask for reasonable
information to verify that you control the account before processing the
request.

## 30-day recovery period

Deletion requests use a 30-day recovery period. During that period:

- The account remains disabled for ordinary use.
- You may sign in to LocTag to reach the recovery screen.
- You may select **Cancel Deletion & Restore** before the displayed deadline.

If you do not cancel before the deadline, permanent deletion processing begins.
Permanent deletion cannot be undone.

## Data deleted after the recovery period

After the 30-day recovery period, LocTag permanently removes the account and
the ordinary product data associated with it, including:

- The LocTag authentication account and profile.
- LocTags created by the account.
- Protected LocTag content associated with those LocTags.
- Stored photos associated with the account and its LocTags.
- Ordinary sharing access and notification registrations tied to the deleted
  account.

## Limited data that may be retained

LocTag may retain limited private deletion-audit, safety, moderation,
fraud-prevention, security, or legally required records for only as long as
reasonably necessary for those purposes. Retained records are not available for
ordinary user access and cannot be used to restore permanently deleted LocTags,
content, or photos.

For more information, review the [LocTag Privacy Policy](privacy-policy.html)
and [LocTag Terms of Use](terms-of-use.html).
